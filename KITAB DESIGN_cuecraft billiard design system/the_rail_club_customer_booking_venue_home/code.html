02 • The Playing Standard

Engineered for serious play & quiet evenings.

Macro Detail: Precision Brass Rail & Diamond Inlays

"Every rail diamond at The Strand Flagship is checked weekly with optical laser micrometers to maintain consistent 51.5mm pocket drops."

01 / CALIBRATION • Tournament Cloth

Simonis 860 Worsted Wool Felt

Combed worsted wool with zero directionality and no pilling. Ironed daily at 60°C to guarantee exact nap speed and clean english application without unwanted table deviation.

02 / BED STRUCTURE • 30mm Italian Slate

Hand-Milled Rasson & Riley Slates

Three-piece diamond-honed slate beds seated on structural I-beams. Micro-adjusted via solid brass leveling screws to within a 0.05mm tolerance across the entire playing surface.

03 / ACOUSTIC ARCHITECTURE • Decibel Isolation

Acoustic Wood Paneling & Task Shading

Slatted American walnut acoustic dampening walls trap high-frequency chatter while letting the crisp ceramic strike of phenolic balls ring true. Custom shaded brass pendants keep glare out of your eyes.
